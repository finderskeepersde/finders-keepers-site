A few things to know.

Shoppers

Where is Finders Keepers Boutique located?

Finders Keepers is located right off Main Street in Newark, Delaware, on the University of Delaware campus. We’re just a 10-minute drive from the Christiana Mall and offer the perk of tax-free shopping in Delaware.

Our boutique is also a short drive from Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New Jersey, making us an easy stop for shoppers across the region looking for curated secondhand fashion. We’re walkable for UD students, convenient for locals, and worth the trip for anyone who loves a good find.

How often do you get new arrivals?

New arrivals hit the floor almost every day. Our inventory moves quickly, especially among students and young professionals looking for trendy, affordable fashion in Newark, DE. To catch the best pieces, stop in often or follow our Instagram for real-time drops, restocks, and try-on hauls.

Do you only buy women’s clothing?

Our main focus is women’s and young contemporary fashion, but we do occasionally offer select unisex and men’s pieces when they fit our curated style. Our goal is always to stock items our shoppers love — stylish, high-quality, and in great condition.

Can I return or exchange items?

Because we’re a small secondhand boutique, all sales are final. We encourage shoppers to try everything on in-store and ask questions before purchasing — our team is always happy to help you find the perfect fit.

What brands do you carry?

We carry a constantly rotating mix of popular mall brands, boutique labels, and higher-end designers. You’ll regularly find favorites like Zara, Aritzia, Lululemon, Free People, Princess Polly, H&M Premium, Anthropologie, and more. Because our inventory is secondhand and curated, no two shopping trips are ever the same — which is part of the fun!

Do you ever run sales?

Yes! While our prices are already curated to be affordable, we occasionally run seasonal sales, student specials, clearance racks, and surprise in-store promotions. Follow us on social media or join our email list so you never miss a deal at Finders Keepers.

Sellers

How does selling to Finders Keepers work?

Want to clean out your closet and get paid cash on the spot? Here’s how our buying process works:

Step 1 — Submit your list
Fill out our selling form. You’ll enter your contact info and list every item you’d like to sell — brand, color, description, size, category, and condition. We require a minimum of 15 pieces per appointment.

Step 2 — Book your appointment
Select an available appointment time directly on the form. Appointments are required — we do not accept walk-ins for selling. Keep an eye on our Instagram stories for when new appointment slots drop, and follow us so you never miss one!

Step 3 — Bring your items in
Come to us at 93 East Main Street, Newark DE at your scheduled time. A member of our team will inspect each item in person for condition and style. We do not accept items with stains, rips, or significant damage.

Step 4 — Get paid
We’ll make you a cash offer on the spot for everything we accept. Items we can’t take will be returned to you, or you can choose to donate them.

Fully booked? Check back soon — we add new appointment slots regularly and announce them first on our Instagram stories. Follow us at @finderskeepersde so you’re always first to know!

What types of items do you buy?

We buy trendy, seasonal, and high-quality women’s clothing, along with select unisex pieces, formalwear, shoes, and accessories. Popular items include denim, dresses, basics, outerwear, activewear, and modern boutique labels. We prioritize currently in-style fashion that appeals to the University of Delaware community and our regional shoppers from the greater Philadelphia area.

What condition should items be in?

We look for items in excellent to like-new condition — clean, odor-free, and free of stains, rips, fading, excessive wear, and pet hair. Clothing should be freshly washed and ready to go straight onto the sales floor. Pieces should feel current, stylish, and something you’d be excited to buy yourself at an upscale secondhand boutique.

How do I get paid?

You’ll receive cash on the spot for any items we accept — no waiting, no consigning, no follow-up needed.
